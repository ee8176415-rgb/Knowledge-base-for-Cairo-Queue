🛂Visa for Exhibitor (+ Supporting Documents)⌄

Exhibitors receive the same invitation letter as visitors — issued on the letterhead of LM AES Victoria (The Landmark Centre) and signed by Ijeoma Urasi — but it is sent together with the supporting documents pack.

  • Participation letter — issued by Informa (Indu Raghavan, Operations Manager)
  • Customs letter — only if the exhibitor is bringing goods / samples

  • Venue tax clearance certificate and its supporting letter

📦Customs Letter (Exhibitor & Pavilion/Agent)⌄

The customs letter supports an exhibitor bringing goods or materials into Nigeria for the show. There are two versions — pick the right one:

  • Exhibitor template — for a company exhibiting on its own stand
  • Agent / Pavilion template — for a company exhibiting as a pavilion (group / country pavilion organiser)

Both letters state that Informa is not the owner or importer of the goods, are issued at the exhibitor's request with no liability to Informa Middle East Limited (Dubai Branch), and ask customs to allow the lawful goods through. Preparation window quoted: Sunday, 25 October to the evening of Thursday, 29 October 2026. Signed by Indu Raghavan, Operations Manager.

Issue the customs letter only when the exhibitor is actually shipping goods, and always fill in the company name and stand number first.

🧰Exhibitor Appointed Contractors (EAC)⌄

An Exhibitor Appointed Contractor (EAC) is any company other than the designated Official Contractor listed in the Customer Centre. This includes:

  • Independent display / installation & dismantling companies
  • Any furnishing supplier that is not one of the Official Vendors listed in the Online Customer Centre
  • Anyone who is not an employee of the exhibitor's company but will work on their booth, equipment or products
  • Delivery personnel, technicians, photographers, florists and anyone hired by the exhibitor who needs access to the exhibit hall
Permission to use an EAC is normally not given for utilities, booth cleaning or material handling — these are exclusive to the venue and/or the official general contractor. The official contractor for this edition is ⟪TBD⟫.

📎 Supporting Documents Pack (exhibitor visa applications)

Sent as email attachments alongside the invitation letter. Document types only are listed here — the files themselves are held internally.

  • Venue company registration (CAC) certificates
  • Venue tax clearance certificate + tax clearance supporting letter
  • VAT registration certificate of the organiser
  • Event management licence of the organiser
  • Passport data page of the authorised signatory
  • Visa-on-arrival price list (internal reference — do not forward to customers)
🔒 Handling rule
  • These files contain third-party personal and company data.
  • Send them only to the exhibitor who requested them, as email attachments.
  • Never publish them, upload them to a public page or repository, post them in a shared channel, or store them outside the approved location.
  • Never copy passport, tax or registration numbers into a case note, a template or this knowledge base.
