⚡ Power
  • Power to all stands is switched on 30 minutes before the show opens and off 30 minutes after it closes every day.
  • If power is needed beyond that, 24hr power must be ordered from the official contractor (Expo Tech).

⚠️ Important notice
  • All stand construction must be completed by 19:00 on Sunday 11 October 2026, including removal of all stand material out of the halls and loading bays.
  • Stand dressing and exhibit set-up must be completed by 22:00 on Sunday 11 October 2026.
  • Stands not completed by 19:00 on Sunday 11 October 2026 are charged a late working fee of 2,000 EGP per extra hour.
  • All contractors and exhibitors must design their stand so it can be built and dismantled within the published times.

ℹ️ Notes
  • Onsite registration is in lobby Hall 3 for FIREX and lobby Hall 4 for Egypt Energy; online pre-registration is on the Customer Center.
  • All visitors, exhibitors and contractors must show a valid ID (driving licence, CPR or passport) on arrival at the EIEC.
  • The source timetable labels these rows Sunday / Tuesday / Wednesday / Thursday — the dates above are the reference (12 Oct 2026 is a Monday).

📦 Dismantling Timings

Stand Dismantle DayTimingsAccess
Wed, 14 Oct 202617:00 – 23:59Shell scheme & wooden stands
Wed, 14 Oct 202620:30 – 23:59Space only contractors
Thu, 15 Oct 202600:01 – 08:00Space only contractors
⚠️ Important notice
  • No exhibits or stand material may be removed before the start of stand dismantle has been publicly announced by the organiser.
  • Exhibits cannot be removed or dismantled before the close of the show, or until the halls are clear of all visitors.
  • Access for space-only contractors is approximate — they are held at the marshalling gate until venue rigging services, aisle carpet removal and empty boxes / crates have been delivered to stands.
  • Exhibitors are advised not to leave their stand unattended at any time during dismantle.
  • It is the exhibitor's responsibility to remove all stand materials, carpet tape, packaging and waste — charges are levied for the removal of discarded items.
  • The organisers and the venue are not responsible for anything left in the halls or loading bays overnight or beyond 17:00 on Wednesday 14 October 2026.
