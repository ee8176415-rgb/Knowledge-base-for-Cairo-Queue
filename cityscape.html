Onsite Registration & Badges

Online pre-registration is on the Customer Center. Onsite registration is at the Hall 1 & 4 Lobby during the registration hours. All visitors, exhibitors and contractors must register, badges must be worn at all times on the exhibition floor, and everyone must show a valid ID (driving licence, CPR or passport) on arrival at the EIEC.

✅

After Registering

Every visitor receives a confirmation email containing the visitor badge right after a successful registration. If they cannot find it, ask them to check Junk / Spam before resending it from the CS inbox.

💰 Onsite Fees for Space-Only Stands

100 EGP / SQMdecoration fee → High Speed

High Speed is the appointed contractor coordinating the entry of space-only contractors during build-up and dismantling. They collect the decoration fee and issue the bracelets that let workers in and out of the hall, and they contact each contractor before build-up.

50 EGP / SQMworkers fee → Expo Tech

A new venue fee, collected by Expo Tech on the first day of build-up from their office in the EIEC, between halls 2 & 3.

⚠️ If another supplier claims to be an official supplier, ask to see their official Informa Markets stamp of approval.

⚡ Power
  • Power to all stands is switched on 30 minutes before the show opens and off 30 minutes after it closes every day.
  • If power is required beyond that, 24hr power must be ordered from the official contractor (Expo Tech).

⚠️ Important notice
  • All stand construction must be completed by 19:00 on Tuesday 29 September 2026, including removal of all stand material out of the halls and loading bays.
  • Stand dressing and exhibit set-up must be completed by 22:00 on Tuesday 29 September 2026.
  • Stands not completed by 19:00 on Tuesday 29 September 2026 are charged a late working fee of 2,000 EGP per extra hour.
  • No car access to the venue on the last day of build-up after 18:00, even with a car pass — use the outside car park instead.
  • Early access requests go to techcse@informa.com.

ℹ️ Registration notice
  • For security purposes all visitors, exhibitors and contractors must register, and badges must be worn at all times on the exhibition floor.
  • Online pre-registration is on the Customer Center; onsite registration is at the Hall 1 & 4 Lobby during the registration hours.
  • On arrival at the EIEC everyone must present a valid ID (driving licence, CPR or passport).

📦 Dismantling Timings

Stand Dismantle DayTimingsAccess
Sat, 3 Oct 202620:00 – 23:59All exhibitors
Sat, 3 Oct 202623:00 – 23:59Space only contractors
Sun, 4 Oct 202600:01 – 10:00Space only contractors
⚠️ Important notice
  • No exhibits or stand material may be removed before the start of stand dismantle has been publicly announced by the organiser.
  • Exhibits cannot be removed or dismantled before the close of the show, or until the halls are clear of all visitors.
  • Access for space-only contractors is approximate — they are held at the marshalling gate until venue rigging services, aisle carpet removal and empty boxes / crates have been delivered to stands.
  • Exhibitors are advised not to leave their stand unattended at any time during dismantle.
  • It is the exhibitor's responsibility to remove all stand materials, carpet tape, packaging and waste — charges are levied for the removal of discarded items.
  • The organisers and the venue are not responsible for any items left in the halls or loading bays overnight or beyond 23:59 on Saturday 3 October 2026.

High Speed contacts each contractor before build-up. The workers fee (50 EGP/SQM) is collected by Expo Tech on the first build-up day, from their office between halls 2 & 3.
