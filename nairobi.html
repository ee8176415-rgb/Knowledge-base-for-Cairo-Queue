🇰🇪Kenya eTA — Entry Requirement⌄

Kenya uses an electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA) instead of a traditional visa for most travellers. Every attendee applies for it themselves online, before travelling.

Apply for the Kenya eTA ↗
CS never applies on the customer's behalf and never collects passport scans by chat. We only supply the invitation letter (and, for exhibitors, the supporting documents pack).

📜Participation Letter & Customs Letter⌄

Two separate letters exist for WHX Nairobi 2026, both issued by Informa Markets (Savitha Abreo, Operations Manager). They are event documents — not part of the visa invitation letter:

  • Participation letter — confirms the company is exhibiting, with its stand number. Preparation commences Monday, 14 September 2026 and ends the evening of Friday, 18 September 2026.
  • Customs letter — addressed to the Director of Customs, Nairobi, for exhibitors bringing goods / samples / leaflets for display and marketing only, to be returned to the country of origin. It quotes the wider window: Sunday, 13 September to the evening of Saturday, 20 September 2026. The participating company remains responsible for all applicable charges.
Always fill in the company name and stand number before sending, and use the customs letter only when the exhibitor is actually shipping goods.

🧰Exhibitor Appointed Contractors (EAC)⌄

An Exhibitor Appointed Contractor (EAC) is any company other than the designated Official Contractor (Pro Expo) listed in the Customer Centre. This includes:

  • Independent display / installation & dismantling companies
  • Any furnishing supplier that is not one of the Official Vendors listed in the Online Customer Centre
  • Anyone who is not an employee of the exhibitor's company but will work on their booth, equipment or products
  • Delivery personnel, technicians, photographers, florists and anyone hired by the exhibitor who needs access to the exhibit hall
Permission to use an EAC cannot be given for utilities, booth cleaning or material handling — these are exclusive to the Kenyatta International Convention Centre and/or the official general contractor, Pro Expo.

Who Should Attend

WHX Nairobi is for healthcare decision-makers across East Africa:

  • Healthcare buyers & procurement professionals
  • Dealers & distributors
  • Manufacturers & suppliers
  • Healthcare providers
  • Laboratory professionals (WHX Labs Nairobi)
  • Other industry decision-makers
📍

Venue

Kenyatta International Convention Centre (KICC), Harambee Avenue, Nairobi, Kenya. Halls in use: Hall 1 (Courtyard) and Hall 2 (Tsavo).
