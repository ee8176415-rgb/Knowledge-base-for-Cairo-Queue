Exhibitors

Registration through the Customer Centre Portal, badges and manuals.

🖥️ Exhibitor Registration — Customer Centre Portal

Exhibitors register their team through the Customer Centre Portal. Guide them through these steps:

Log in to the Customer Centre Portal and open the Badges / QR Codes tab.
Check General Booth Information — booth number, size and total badge allotments (e.g. 28 badges).
Under Registered Badges, select the booth from the dropdown.
Use Register Single Member or Register Multiple Members to add the team.
Use Export Badges to download, or Request Extra Badges if more are needed.
Customer Centre Portal — Badges / QR Codes → Registered Badges

Carpeting / Floor Coverings

Shell Scheme: exhibitors are not allowed to change the colour of the carpet.

Space Only: all Space Only stands must install a raised platform for cable management to avoid cable ramps. General height must not exceed 100 mm and must include a ramp for disabled access.

Entertaining on Your Stand

If hosting a party, contact the CS team first — we confirm with the Operation Team before advising the exhibitor to fill in the On Stand Activity Form.
