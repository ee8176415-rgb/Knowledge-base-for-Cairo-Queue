🛂Visa for Exhibitor (+ Supporting Documents)⌄

The exhibitor visa invitation letter is issued manually and must be submitted together with the supporting documents — this applies to exhibitors only. Collect the attendee's details (full name, passport no., passport expiry, place of issue, DOB, arrival/departure dates, company name & address, position, stand number) and prepare the official invitation letter.

Unlike visitors, there is no automated exhibitor visa link for this edition — the letter + supporting documents are provided by the team.

Case categories (Salesforce):

📧 Email template — Exhibitor visa invitation + supporting documents
Dear [Name],

Kindly find attached your official invitation letter for WHX Johannesburg 2026, together with the supporting documents required for your visa application.

Please submit the invitation letter along with the attached supporting documents as part of your visa application. Ensure all details on the letter (name, passport number, dates and stand number) match your travel document exactly, and let us know if anything needs to be amended.

Should you require any further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.
