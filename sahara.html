🖥️ Exhibitor Registration — Customer Centre Portal

Exhibitors register their team through the Customer Centre Portal. Guide them through these steps:

Log in to the Customer Centre Portal and open the Badges / QR Codes tab.
Check General Booth Information — booth number, size and total badge allotments.
Under Registered Badges, select the booth from the dropdown.
Use Register Single Member or Register Multiple Members to add the team.
Use Export Badges to download, or Request Extra Badges if more are needed.
